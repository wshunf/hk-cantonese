常見問題

發音評分好似唔準？

請先做一次「聲調校準」（設定 → 聲調校準）。每個人音域唔同，校準之後評分會用返你自己把聲做基準，唔會因為你把聲低沉或者高音而扣錯分。

另外評分靠語音識別，環境嘈、講得太細聲、或者夾雜英文都會影響結果。喺安靜環境用正常音量讀一次會準好多。

錄音冇反應／收唔到聲

去 iOS「設定 → 港廣東話」，確認「麥克風」同「語音識別」兩樣都開咗。如果啱啱先開，可能要重開一次 app。

買咗完整版，但換咗機／重裝之後唔見咗

喺 app 入面去「設定 → 完整版 → 回復購買」，用返當初購買嗰個 Apple 帳戶就會恢復。呢個係一次性買斷，唔會過期。

點樣唔顯示譯文？

設定 → 教材譯文 → 譯文語言，揀「－」就得。噉樣介面同教材全部只顯示廣東話，適合想沉浸式學嘅人。想睇返譯文隨時揀返英文／日文／韓文／越南文。

聽落把聲讀錯字？

部分畫面用系統語音合成朗讀，個別字（尤其多音字）可能會讀錯。會話部分用嘅係預先錄好嘅音檔，準確好多。如果你發現有明顯讀錯嘅地方，歡迎電郵話我知邊一句，我會修正。

點樣清除學習記錄？

設定 → 學習進度 → 重設學習進度。呢個操作冇得返轉頭，會清走所有學過嘅單元、發音分數同統計。

廣告可唔可以唔顯示？

購買完整版之後就唔會再有廣告。喺歐洲經濟區、英國同瑞士，你亦可以喺「設定 → 私隱 → 廣告私隱設定」更改廣告同意選擇。

Frequently asked questions

Pronunciation scores seem wrong

Run Tone calibration first (Settings → Tone calibration). Everyone's vocal range differs; after calibrating, scoring uses your own voice as the reference instead of penalising you for a naturally low or high pitch.

Scoring also relies on speech recognition, so background noise, speaking too quietly, or mixing in English will affect results. A quiet room and a normal speaking volume make a big difference.

Recording doesn't respond

In iOS Settings → HK Cantonese, make sure both Microphone and Speech Recognition are enabled. If you just granted them, restart the app.

I bought the full version but lost it after reinstalling

Go to Settings → Full version → Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. It is a one-time purchase and never expires.

How do I hide translations?

Settings → Translations → Translation language, choose "－". Everything then shows in Cantonese only, which suits immersive study. Switch back to English, Japanese, Korean or Vietnamese any time.

A word is read out incorrectly

Some screens use system speech synthesis, which can mispronounce certain characters (especially heteronyms). Dialogues use pre-recorded audio and are far more accurate. If you spot a clear error, email me the sentence and I'll fix it.

How do I clear my learning records?

Settings → Learning progress → Reset learning progress. This cannot be undone.

Can I remove ads?

Yes — buying the full version removes them permanently. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can also change your advertising consent under Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy settings.
