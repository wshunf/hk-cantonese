港廣東話 — 私隱權政策

最後更新：2026 年 8 月 20 日

「港廣東話」（下稱「本應用程式」）係一個學習香港廣東話嘅 iOS 應用程式。我哋重視你嘅私隱，本政策說明本應用程式會處理啲乜嘢資料、點解要處理，以及交畀邊啲第三方。

本應用程式冇任何帳戶或註冊功能，唔會收集你嘅姓名、電郵地址、電話號碼或者其他可以直接識別你身分嘅資料。

1. 儲存喺你部裝置上嘅資料

以下資料只會存喺你部裝置本機，我哋（開發者）睇唔到、亦攞唔到：

  • 學習進度（學過邊啲單元、練習次數）
  • 發音練習分數同聲調校準結果
  • AI 對話記錄
  • 你嘅設定（譯文語言、朗讀速度、外觀模式等）

如果你喺 iOS 開咗 iCloud 備份，呢啲檔案可能會隨你部裝置嘅備份一齊上傳到你自己嘅 iCloud，該備份由 Apple 按其私隱政策處理。

刪除方法：喺應用程式「設定 → 重設學習進度」可以清除學習記錄；刪除應用程式會一併移除全部本機資料。

2. 會傳送出去嘅資料

Groq, Inc.（api.groq.com，美國） 你喺「AI 複習」打或者用語音講嘅文字，連同學習進度摘要（學過邊啲單元同次數、發音練習次數同平均分、發音弱項嘅字詞同得分）。要你喺應用程式內明確同意咗先會傳送 即時生成複習題目同批改答案

2.1 麥克風同語音識別

本應用程式喺你主動撳錄音掣嗰陣先會使用麥克風，用作發音評分同語音輸入。錄音檔案唔會儲存喺裝置上，亦唔會傳送畀開發者。

不過請注意：語音轉文字係使用 Apple 內建嘅語音識別服務，iOS 可能會將語音內容傳送到 Apple 嘅伺服器處理（視乎你部裝置同系統設定）。呢部分由 Apple 按其私隱政策處理。

2.2 AI 功能

「複習」分頁嘅「AI 複習」會將你打或者用語音講嘅文字，連同你嘅學習進度摘要（學過邊啲單元同次數、發音練習次數同平均分、發音弱項嘅字詞同得分），傳送到 Groq, Inc.（美國）嘅伺服器，用嚟即時生成複習題目同批改答案。

第一次用之前，應用程式會先出一版說明，列明會傳送啲乜、傳送畀邊個、用嚟做咩；你撳咗「同意並開始」之後，先至會傳送任何資料。唔同意嘅話，應用程式其他所有功能照樣用得。你亦可以隨時喺「設定 → AI 複習 → 將資料送去 Groq」撤回同意，撤回之後就唔會再傳送任何資料。

Groq 只會用你嘅內容即時生成回覆，唔會攞去訓練模型（依 Groq 商用 API 條款），其餘按 Groq 嘅私隱政策處理。你嘅錄音本身唔會傳送畀 Groq —— 語音輸入會先經 Apple 語音識別轉成文字，只有文字會傳送。請唔好喺 AI 對話入面輸入敏感個人資料（例如身分證號碼、銀行資料、健康狀況）。

2.3 廣告

本版本（1.0）唔含任何廣告。應用程式唔會存取廣告識別碼（IDFA），亦唔會同任何廣告聯播網分享資料；應用程式嘅私隱清單（privacy manifest）聲明 NSPrivacyTracking = false。

日後如果加返廣告，我哋會先更新本政策，並喺應用程式內另行徵求你嘅同意。

3. 我哋唔會做嘅嘢

  • 唔會出售你嘅個人資料
  • 冇使用第三方分析或當機回報服務
  • 唔會將你嘅學習資料或對話記錄上傳到我哋自己嘅伺服器（我哋根本冇伺服器）

4. 兒童

本應用程式並非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，亦唔會有意收集兒童嘅個人資料。

5. 你嘅權利

視乎你所在地區（例如歐盟 GDPR、英國 UK GDPR、加州 CCPA），你可能有權查閱、更正或刪除你嘅個人資料，以及撤回同意。由於本應用程式唔設帳戶、資料主要存喺你自己部裝置，你可以直接透過上述「刪除方法」自行行使；如涉及 AI 功能嘅同意，可以隨時喺應用程式「設定 → AI 複習」撤回。如有其他要求，可以用下面嘅電郵聯絡我哋。

  • Groq：groq.com/privacy-policy
  • Apple：apple.com/legal/privacy

7. 政策更新

本政策如有修訂，會喺呢一版更新，並修改上方嘅「最後更新」日期。

HK Cantonese — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

HK Cantonese ("the App") is an iOS app for learning Hong Kong Cantonese. This policy explains what data the App processes, why, and which third parties receive it.

The App has no account or sign-up. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other information that directly identifies you.

1. Data stored on your device

The following stays on your device. We (the developer) cannot see or retrieve it:

  • Learning progress (units studied, practice counts)
  • Pronunciation scores and tone calibration results
  • AI conversation history
  • Your settings (translation language, speech rate, appearance)

If you have iCloud Backup enabled, these files may be included in your own device backup, which Apple handles under its privacy policy.

Deletion: Use "Settings → Reset learning progress" in the App to clear your records. Deleting the App removes all local data.

2. Data sent off the device

2.1 Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used only when you actively start a recording, for pronunciation scoring and voice input. Recordings are not stored on your device and are never sent to the developer.

Please note that speech-to-text uses Apple's built-in speech recognition, and iOS may send the audio to Apple's servers for processing, depending on your device and system settings. That processing is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

2.2 AI features

AI Review (in the Review tab) sends the text you type or dictate, together with a summary of your learning progress (units studied and how often, practice count and average score, weak pronunciation items with their scores), to the servers of Groq, Inc. (USA), which generates your review questions and checks your answers.

Before this happens for the first time, the App shows a disclosure screen naming exactly what is sent, who it is sent to and why; nothing is sent until you tap "Agree and start". If you decline, every other feature of the App remains fully usable. You can withdraw consent at any time under "Settings → AI Review → Send data to Groq", after which nothing further is sent.

Groq uses your content only to generate the reply in real time and does not use it to train models (per Groq's commercial API terms); the rest is governed by Groq's privacy policy. Your audio is never sent to Groq — voice input is first transcribed by Apple's speech recognition, and only the resulting text is sent. Please do not enter sensitive personal information (such as identity numbers, banking details, or health information) into AI conversations.

2.3 Advertising

This version (1.0) contains no advertising. The App does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA) and shares no data with any ad network; its privacy manifest declares NSPrivacyTracking = false.

If advertising is added in a future version, this policy will be updated first and consent will be requested separately in the App.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data
  • We use no third-party analytics or crash-reporting services
  • We do not upload your learning data or conversations to our own servers (we operate none)

4. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. EU GDPR, UK GDPR, California CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because the App has no accounts and data is kept on your own device, you can exercise these directly using the deletion steps above; for the AI feature, you can withdraw consent at any time under "Settings → AI Review" in the App. For anything else, contact us at the address below.

6. Third-party privacy policies

  • Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy

Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
