港廣東話 — 私隱權政策

最後更新：2026 年 7 月 29 日

「港廣東話」（下稱「本應用程式」）係一個學習香港廣東話嘅 iOS 應用程式。我哋重視你嘅私隱，本政策說明本應用程式會處理啲乜嘢資料、點解要處理，以及交畀邊啲第三方。

本應用程式冇任何帳戶或註冊功能，唔會收集你嘅姓名、電郵地址、電話號碼或者其他可以直接識別你身分嘅資料。

1. 儲存喺你部裝置上嘅資料

以下資料只會存喺你部裝置本機，我哋（開發者）睇唔到、亦攞唔到：

  • 學習進度（學過邊啲單元、練習次數）
  • 發音練習分數同聲調校準結果
  • AI 對話記錄
  • 你嘅設定（譯文語言、朗讀速度、外觀模式等）

如果你喺 iOS 開咗 iCloud 備份，呢啲檔案可能會隨你部裝置嘅備份一齊上傳到你自己嘅 iCloud，該備份由 Apple 按其私隱政策處理。

刪除方法：喺應用程式「設定 → 重設學習進度」可以清除學習記錄；刪除應用程式會一併移除全部本機資料。

2. 會傳送出去嘅資料

接收方傳送咩用途
Groq（api.groq.com） 你喺 AI 功能打嘅文字；使用「AI 複習」時，會連同你嘅學習進度摘要（例如學過幾多單元、練習平均分）一齊傳送 產生 AI 導師嘅回覆
Apple 語音識別 你錄音嘅語音內容 將你把聲轉成文字，用嚟評分同語音輸入
Google AdMob 裝置及廣告相關識別資料；如你透過 iOS 追蹤許可（ATT）同意，會包括廣告識別碼（IDFA） 顯示廣告、量度成效、防止廣告詐騙
Apple（App Store） 購買交易由 Apple 處理 處理一次性內購（解鎖完整版）

2.1 麥克風同語音識別

本應用程式喺你主動撳錄音掣嗰陣先會使用麥克風，用作發音評分同語音輸入。錄音檔案唔會儲存喺裝置上，亦唔會傳送畀開發者。

不過請注意：語音轉文字係使用 Apple 內建嘅語音識別服務，iOS 可能會將語音內容傳送到 Apple 嘅伺服器處理（視乎你部裝置同系統設定）。呢部分由 Apple 按其私隱政策處理。

2.2 AI 功能

你喺 AI 功能輸入嘅文字會傳送到 Groq 嘅伺服器處理，並由 Groq 按其私隱政策處理。請唔好喺 AI 對話入面輸入敏感個人資料（例如身分證號碼、銀行資料、健康狀況）。

2.3 廣告

本應用程式使用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告。購買完整版之後就唔會再顯示廣告。

喺歐洲經濟區、英國及瑞士，我哋會喺顯示廣告前透過 Google 嘅同意管理平台（UMP）徵求你嘅同意；你可以隨時喺應用程式「設定 → 私隱 → 廣告私隱設定」更改或撤回你嘅選擇。喺 iOS 上，你亦可以喺「系統設定 → 私隱與安全性 → 追蹤」控制追蹤許可。

3. 我哋唔會做嘅嘢

  • 唔會出售你嘅個人資料
  • 冇使用第三方分析或當機回報服務
  • 唔會將你嘅學習資料或對話記錄上傳到我哋自己嘅伺服器（我哋根本冇伺服器）

4. 兒童

本應用程式並非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，亦唔會有意收集兒童嘅個人資料。

5. 你嘅權利

視乎你所在地區（例如歐盟 GDPR、英國 UK GDPR、加州 CCPA），你可能有權查閱、更正或刪除你嘅個人資料，以及撤回同意。由於本應用程式唔設帳戶、資料主要存喺你自己部裝置，你可以直接透過上述「刪除方法」自行行使；如涉及廣告同意，請使用應用程式內嘅「廣告私隱設定」。如有其他要求，可以用下面嘅電郵聯絡我哋。

  • Google（AdMob）：policies.google.com/privacy
  • Groq：groq.com/privacy-policy
  • Apple：apple.com/legal/privacy

7. 政策更新

本政策如有修訂，會喺呢一版更新，並修改上方嘅「最後更新」日期。

HK Cantonese — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

HK Cantonese ("the App") is an iOS app for learning Hong Kong Cantonese. This policy explains what data the App processes, why, and which third parties receive it.

The App has no account or sign-up. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other information that directly identifies you.

1. Data stored on your device

The following stays on your device. We (the developer) cannot see or retrieve it:

  • Learning progress (units studied, practice counts)
  • Pronunciation scores and tone calibration results
  • AI conversation history
  • Your settings (translation language, speech rate, appearance)

If you have iCloud Backup enabled, these files may be included in your own device backup, which Apple handles under its privacy policy.

Deletion: Use "Settings → Reset learning progress" in the App to clear your records. Deleting the App removes all local data.

2. Data sent off the device

RecipientWhat is sentPurpose
Groq (api.groq.com) Text you type into the AI features. When using AI Review, a summary of your learning progress (e.g. units studied, average practice score) is included Generating the AI tutor's replies
Apple Speech Recognition The speech you record Converting speech to text for scoring and voice input
Google AdMob Device and ad-related identifiers; includes the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you consent via iOS App Tracking Transparency Serving ads, measuring performance, preventing ad fraud
Apple (App Store) Purchases are processed by Apple Handling the one-time in-app purchase

2.1 Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used only when you actively start a recording, for pronunciation scoring and voice input. Recordings are not stored on your device and are never sent to the developer.

Please note that speech-to-text uses Apple's built-in speech recognition, and iOS may send the audio to Apple's servers for processing, depending on your device and system settings. That processing is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

2.2 AI features

Text you enter in the AI features is sent to Groq's servers and processed under Groq's privacy policy. Please do not enter sensitive personal information (such as identity numbers, banking details, or health information) into AI conversations.

2.3 Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob. Ads are removed permanently once you buy the full version.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, we request your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before serving ads. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time via "Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy settings" in the App. On iOS you can also control tracking under "Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking".

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data
  • We use no third-party analytics or crash-reporting services
  • We do not upload your learning data or conversations to our own servers (we operate none)

4. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. EU GDPR, UK GDPR, California CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because the App has no accounts and data is kept on your own device, you can exercise these directly using the deletion steps above; for advertising consent, use "Ad privacy settings" in the App. For anything else, contact us at the address below.

6. Third-party privacy policies

  • Google (AdMob): policies.google.com/privacy
  • Groq: groq.com/privacy-policy
  • Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy

Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
